How it works

Three taps from "I'm going to Italy" to the plan you'll buy.

  1. Pick the country and your datesType a country, then tap the day you land and the day you leave.
  2. Say how much data you needAny amount, 3 GB, 10 GB or unlimited, local or multi-country plans.
  3. Compare and buy from the providerSimScout ranks the plans; tap one to open the provider's own site and buy there.

About SimScout

An independent comparison app made by a traveller, for travellers.

How SimScout makes money

SimScout is free. Some links to providers are affiliate links: if you buy after tapping one, SimScout may earn a small commission, at no extra cost to you.

Commissions never change the order of the results. The ranking is only what you choose: price, data, quality, popularity or rating.

Your privacy

No sign-up and no account. Your trips, saved plans and eSIM history stay on your phone and are never sent to us.

This website (not the app) uses a script from our affiliate partner Travelpayouts to track referrals.

Prices change often, so always confirm the final price on the provider's site before you pay.
